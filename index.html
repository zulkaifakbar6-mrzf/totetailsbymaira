01 • Our Story

A little bag. A lot of you.

Interview sheet — five essential questions
  1. Would you buy a reusable customized canvas bag? Why / why not?
  2. Which collection would you choose: Quote, Name, Art or Memory?
  3. What size, colour and print quality would you expect?
  4. What is the highest price you would realistically pay?
  5. How would you order and receive it? What would stop you?
Supply model — choose after sample testing
A

Outsource production

Buy plain bags and pay a printer / hand-painter per order. Lower setup, vendor dependency.

B

Hire a bag maker

Contract a maker on a per-piece fee. Cost, quality, lead time and minimum quantity must be agreed.

Decision rule: compare 2–3 samples for stitching, fabric, print hold, price and lead time.

  4. Student referrals & stall — test discount and pop-up conversion.

Pilot KPIs — what success means
  • Research: interview count and willingness to pay
  • Demand: genuine preorders & conversion rate
  • Production: cost per bag, reworks and lead time
  • Customers: complaints, satisfaction & repeat interest
  • Economics: contribution per bag & pilot payback
SWOT — balanced, no unrealistic claims

Strengths

Personal / reusable design; small initial stock.

Weaknesses

Manual work can be slow; material-dependent quality.

Opportunities

Gift demand; student events; pouches / caps later.

Threats

Cheaper rivals; changing tastes; production delays.
